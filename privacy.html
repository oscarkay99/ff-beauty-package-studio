Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

FF Beauty Package Studio ("we," "our," or "the studio") operates this website to share information about our services, showcase our work, and make it easy to reach us. This page explains what happens to your information when you visit.

The short version

This website does not have sign-ups, accounts, online payments, or contact forms. It doesn't run analytics or advertising trackers. The only thing stored in your browser is a small preference so we don't show you the cookie notice twice.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through this website. There is nothing to fill out and submit. If you call the phone number listed on this site, that call is handled by your phone carrier and is not something this website collects or stores.

Cookies & Local Storage

This site uses your browser's local storage for one purpose: remembering that you've seen and dismissed the cookie notice. It does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies. You can clear this at any time through your browser's settings, and the notice will simply appear again on your next visit.

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Loading a font, like loading any resource from a server, means your browser makes a request that includes standard technical information such as your IP address. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, not by us.

Third-Party Links

This site links out to Instagram, Facebook, and Sisterlocks' official website. Once you click through, you're on their platform and subject to their own privacy practices, which we don't control.

Photos on This Site

Photos and video featured in the gallery and elsewhere on this site are of real clients, used with their permission to showcase our work. If you're a client and would like a photo of you removed, contact us using the information below and we'll take care of it.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If anything about how this site works changes, this page will be updated with a new "last updated" date above.
